# data/RAG-md-mouse/

This directory should contain the Markdown-converted full text of the 8 mouse papers
listed below. Full text is not redistributed in this repository due to publisher
copyright — obtain each paper yourself (e.g. via your institution's journal access) and
convert it to Markdown using a PDF-to-Markdown tool of your choice, then place the result
here using the naming pattern `<First author> et al - <year> - <short title>.md`
(matching the filenames already referenced in `output/`).

1. Gao, L. et al. Single-neuron projectome of mouse prefrontal cortex. *Nat Neurosci* 25, 515–529 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-022-01041-5
2. Qiu, S. et al. Whole-brain spatial organization of hippocampal single-neuron projectomes. *Science* 383, eadj9198 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adj9198
3. Dou, Y. et al. Single-neuron projectome-guided analysis reveals the neural circuit mechanism underlying endogenous opioid antinociception. *National Science Review* 11, nwae195 (2024). https://academic.oup.com/nsr/article/11/7/nwae195/7687829
4. Li, X. et al. Single-neuron projectomes of mouse paraventricular hypothalamic nucleus oxytocin neurons reveal mutually exclusive projection patterns. *Neuron* (2024). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38290516/
5. Ji, X. et al. Activation of hypothalamic-pontine-spinal pathway promotes locomotor initiation and functional recovery after spinal cord injury in mice. *Nat Commun* (2025). https://www.nature.com/nature-index/article/10.1038/s41467-025-67133-5
6. Jiao, Z. et al. Projectome-based characterization of hypothalamic peptidergic neurons in male mice. *Nat Neurosci* 28, 1073–1088 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-025-01919-0
7. Ding, W. et al. Single-neuron projectome reveals organization of somatosensory ascending pathways in the mouse brain. *Neuron* (2025). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40209714/
8. Gao, L. et al. Integrative analysis of single-neuron projectomes links connectome, transcriptome, and function in the mouse cortex. *Neuron* (2026). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41253150/
