# Source Papers

The literature corpus used to build the RAG knowledge base (`data/RAG-md-mouse/` and
`data/RAG-md-macaque/` referenced throughout this pipeline) consists of full text from the
11 published papers listed below. **Paper full text is not redistributed in this
repository** due to publisher copyright; only the source papers are listed here. To
reproduce the knowledge base, obtain each paper (e.g. via your institution's journal
access) and convert it to Markdown using a PDF-to-Markdown tool of your choice, then place
the resulting files under `data/RAG-md-mouse/` or `data/RAG-md-macaque/` following the
naming pattern `<First author> et al - <year> - <short title>.md`.
