Type: single-evidence
Source:

  - Evidence 1: "Cluster 1 (C1) contained reconstructed axons of 177 neurons terminating in ME; cluster 2 (C2) comprised axons of 87 neurons with more extensive arborizations, terminating in the forebrain, midbrain, pons, cerebellum, and medulla, but without terminals in ME."

Type: single-evidence
Source:
  - Evidence 1: "Together, the above results show that magnocellular OXT marker Calb1 and parvocellular OXT marker Reln also serve as markers of C1 and C2 neurons, respectively."
